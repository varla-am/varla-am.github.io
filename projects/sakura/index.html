Sakura

macOS browser · Swift 1.0.1

A minimal, private browser for macOS.

DownloadSource

About

Sakura puts your tabs in a sidebar, blocks trackers and does not keep your browsing history.

What it does

  • Sakura Sidebar: an Arc-style sidebar with vertical tabs and favorites
  • Sakura TrackerBlock: blocks trackers and ads and counts how many it stopped
  • Safari Web Extensions: runs the extensions you already use in Safari
  • Sakura PassMan: a password manager with AutoFill and CSV import from Apple Passwords or Chrome
  • Privacy: Sakura never stores your browsing history

Requirements

  • macOS 26 Tahoe or newer
  • Apple Silicon or Intel
  • Xcode 26 or newer to build from source

Install

  1. Download Sakura-v1.0.1.dmg from Releases.
  2. Open it and drag Sakura into Applications.
  3. Gatekeeper may block the first launch because the app is not notarized: right click, Open.
