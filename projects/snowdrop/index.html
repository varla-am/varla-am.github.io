Snowdrop

macOS · Swift 1.2

A minimal YouTube Music and YouTube player for the Mac, forked from Kaset.

DownloadSource

About

Snowdrop keeps the clean, Apple Music-like layout of Kaset and adds a full YouTube mode, Shorts, a floating player, themes and automatic updates. Everything lives in one native window with a floating glass sidebar and player bar.

What it does

  • YouTube Music: recommendations organized like the original client, in a floating glass sidebar
  • YouTube mode: switch with one button at the bottom of the sidebar, with topic chips and a grid like the real home page
  • Shorts: a vertical player with like, dislike, share and next/previous buttons
  • Floating player: an overlay that shows what is playing, a song or a video, and lets you control it
  • Subtitles: a CC button in the player bar. Click to toggle, right-click to pick a language
  • Keys: K play/pause, J and L skip 10 seconds, C captions, F fullscreen
  • Themes with adjustable window transparency and blur
  • Updates itself: checks for new releases and installs them in one click

Requirements

  • macOS 15.4 or later (Apple Intelligence features need macOS 26)
  • A Google account for signed-in playback

Install

  1. Download the latest .dmg from Releases.
  2. Open it and drag Snowdrop to Applications.
  3. The app is not notarized: right click it and choose Open the first time.

Snowdrop is a fork of Kaset by sozercan, licensed under MIT. It is an unofficial client: Google does not support it.
