Snowdrop

macOS · Swift

A minimal YouTube Music and YouTube player for the Mac, forked from Kaset.

  • YouTube Music: recommendations organized like the original client, in a floating glass sidebar
  • YouTube mode: switch with one button at the bottom of the sidebar, with topic chips and a grid like the real home page
  • Shorts: a vertical player with like, dislike, share and next/previous buttons

Blossom

macOS · Swift

A tiny Spotlight-style launcher: press a hotkey, type a command, and things happen.

  • Commands: toggle light and dark mode, screensaver, sleep, lock screen, logout, volume and more
  • Web search: search cats opens the results in your browser. Pick Google, DuckDuckGo, Bing, Brave, Ecosia, Startpage, Yandex or your own address
  • Three themes: Aqua, Default (Big Sur to Sequoia) and Liquid Glass
